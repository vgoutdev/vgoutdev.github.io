{%- comment -%} Prints the URL of a page in the current language (or include.lang). Usage: URLs follow the permalinks of the pages: English at //, others at ///, and "home" at / or //. (Looking pages up through site.pages works too, but makes the build many times slower.) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _lang = include.lang | default: page.lang | default: "en" -%} {%- capture _url -%} {%- unless _lang == "en" -%}/{{ _lang }}{%- endunless -%}/{%- unless include.ref == "home" -%}{{ include.ref }}/{%- endunless -%} {%- endcapture -%} {%- if include.absolute -%}{{- _url | absolute_url -}}{%- else -%}{{- _url | relative_url -}}{%- endif -%}
